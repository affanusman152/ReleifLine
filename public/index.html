The problem

In floods, earthquakes and conflicts, cries for help pour into WhatsApp as panicked voice notes in many languages. Volunteers copy them into spreadsheets by hand while people wait.

What ReliefLine does

  1. Listens: voice notes are transcribed (Whisper), in any language.
  2. Understands: AI extracts the need, the people, vulnerable groups and the location, and suggests a priority.
  3. Spots duplicates: reports within 300 m are flagged as possibly the same emergency.
  4. A human decides: a coordinator verifies, confirms or overrides the priority, merges duplicates or rejects false reports.
  5. Dispatches: the nearest available volunteer gets the task on WhatsApp with a map link.
  6. Closes the loop: the requester is told "help is on the way" in their own language.

Why a human must decide

Life-or-death triage needs accountability, local knowledge and protection against false or manipulated reports. No human can listen to 5,000 voice notes in 10 languages, and no AI should decide alone who gets rescued first. Every AI suggestion and every human decision is in the Audit log.

Not a replacement for 1122 / 112 / 911

Emergency numbers handle one emergency at a time. ReliefLine is the surge layer for the day a thousand people need help at once, and for the NGOs and volunteers who do much of the rescuing.
